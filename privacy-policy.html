Privacy Policy — Risk Position Size Calculator

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Risk Position Size Calculator is a Chrome extension that calculates trading position sizes. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What is stored

The extension saves only what you enter so it is there next time: your calculator settings per symbol (capital, risk %, leverage, fees, slippage, maintenance margin rate, funding rate and interval, quantity decimals) and your latest 100 calculation records.

Where it is stored

This data is stored with Chrome's built-in storage (chrome.storage.sync) in your own Chrome profile. If Chrome sync is turned on, Google syncs it between your own devices under your Google account, as described in Google's privacy policy. The developer never receives it.

Network access

The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads no remote code.

Deleting your data

Delete records in the extension (✕ or "Clear all"), or remove the extension to delete all of its stored data.

Contact

Please use the support / contact option on this extension's Chrome Web Store page.

隱私權政策 — 風險倉位計算器

最後更新:2026-11-01

風險倉位計算器是一個計算交易倉位大小的 Chrome 擴充功能。本擴充功能不會收集、傳送、出售或分享任何個人資料。

儲存哪些資料

只會保存你輸入的內容,方便下次使用:各標的的計算設定(總資金、風險 %、槓桿、手續費、滑點、維持保證金率、資金費率與結算間隔、數量小數位),以及最近 100 筆計算紀錄。

存在哪裡

資料使用 Chrome 內建儲存(chrome.storage.sync)存在你自己的 Chrome 設定檔中。若你開啟 Chrome 同步,Google 會依其隱私權政策在你自己的裝置之間同步。開發者不會收到這些資料。

網路存取

本擴充功能不發出任何網路請求,不使用分析或追蹤工具,也不載入遠端程式碼。

刪除資料

可在擴充功能中刪除紀錄(✕ 或「全部清除」),或移除擴充功能以刪除所有已儲存的資料。

聯絡方式

請透過本擴充功能在 Chrome 線上應用程式商店頁面上的支援 / 聯絡選項與我們聯繫。
